StormProof Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Alex Floyd

StormProof: Home Inventory helps you photograph and document the contents of your home and export a claims-ready PDF for your insurer. It is built so that your inventory never leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. StormProof has no account, no sign-in, no server and no analytics. The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal information off your device.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter (property details, rooms, items, values, photos, receipts and serial-plate photos) is stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iOS device backup if you have backups enabled, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera access to photograph rooms and items, and for photo-library access only when you choose to attach an existing photo. Photos are processed and stored on your device. You can revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Exported reports

When you export a PDF report, it is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where you send it (email, Files, AirDrop, your insurer's portal) is entirely your choice; StormProof never sees or keeps a copy.

Purchases

The optional StormProof Complete upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. StormProof only receives confirmation that the purchase is active and never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you turn on the yearly export reminder in Settings, the app schedules a single local notification on your device. No notification service or server is involved.

Children

StormProof is not directed at children under 13 and, because it collects no information, does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
