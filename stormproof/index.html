StormProof: Home Inventory

Photograph your home room by room, record what you own, and export a timestamped, claims-ready PDF for your insurance company before the next storm. Everything stays on your phone.

Frequently asked

Is my inventory backed up? StormProof stores everything locally on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the device. Export a PDF and keep a copy somewhere safe (email it to yourself or save it to Files) so you have it even if your phone is lost in a storm.

What does StormProof Complete include? Unlimited rooms, receipt attachments and reports without the watermark. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings on a new phone.

How do I delete my data? Delete rooms or items inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
